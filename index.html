Garbi

A personal AI assistant that automates my own digital life.

What it is

Garbi is a personal AI assistant I (Miquel Poch) built and run on my own computer. It connects to a small set of my personal accounts to automate routine tasks I would otherwise do by hand.

What it does

  • Reads my email to monitor specific workflows (vacation expense tracking, service alerts).
  • Sends the occasional email on my behalf (status reports, briefings) when I ask it to.
  • Reads and updates events in my personal Google Calendar.
  • Reads and writes specific files in my Google Drive (project documents I create myself).
  • Controls a few smart-home devices on my local network.

Garbi is a single-user tool. I am the only person who uses it, the only person whose data it touches, and the only person who runs it.
