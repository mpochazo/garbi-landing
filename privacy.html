Privacy Policy

Garbi · Last updated 11 October 2026 (revised)

Summary

Garbi is a personal AI assistant I (Miquel Poch) built and run on my own computer. It only ever touches my own data, only ever acts on my own accounts, and only ever runs on my own hardware. It is not a service available to anyone else. This page describes what data it accesses, why, and how that data is handled.

Who is the data controller?

Miquel Poch, based in Alicante, Spain. Contact: tabarcloudsolutions@gmail.com.

What data Garbi accesses

Garbi connects to a small set of Google services using OAuth, with the following scopes:

  • Gmail (read & send) — to monitor specific email threads I have set up (e.g. a vacation-expense thread, a service-alert thread) and to send status reports when I explicitly ask it to.
  • Google Calendar — to read my events so it can flag conflicts, and to add or update events only when I explicitly ask it to.
  • Google Drive — read/write access to specific files I create (such as design documents or report exports). Garbi cannot see, list, or touch the rest of my Drive.
  • Google Docs — read/write access to documents I explicitly tell it to create or update.

All four integrations are tied to a single Google account: miquel.poch@gmail.com.

Why Garbi accesses it

Each integration exists to replace a small piece of manual work I would otherwise do myself: tracking expenses from receipt photos, summarizing emails, drafting recurring reports, syncing a personal calendar. None of this data is sold, shared, profiled, or used for advertising.

Where the data lives

Garbi runs on a single personal computer in my home. Tokens and cached email/calendar metadata are stored locally in encrypted files on that computer. They are not uploaded to any cloud service I operate. The underlying language-model API calls (used to summarize or draft content) are sent to a third-party LLM provider; only the specific content of the task I am working on is transmitted, not my entire mailbox or calendar.

Who else sees the data

  • Google — as the provider of Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Docs. Their own privacy policy applies to data stored with them.
  • Language-model provider — the prompt and relevant context Garbi sends when I invoke a model for summarization, drafting, or classification. I do not send advertising IDs, contact lists, or full mailboxes.

Beyond those two, nobody. I do not share Garbi output with third parties.

How long data is kept

Cached email/calendar metadata and generated reports are kept locally until I delete them. I review and prune the workspace periodically. Google-side data is governed by my own Google account retention settings.

Your rights (GDPR / LOPDGDD)

Since Garbi only ever processes my own personal data, there is no other data subject to notify. If you are reading this because you received an email or message that appears to come from Garbi, it was sent by me, manually or as part of a workflow I triggered. You can ask me at any time to:

  • stop sending you messages,
  • tell you what data of yours is in my systems,
  • delete any such data.

Reach me at tabarcloudsolutions@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If I add a new integration or change what data Garbi touches, I will update this page and bump the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the changelog on the homepage.
